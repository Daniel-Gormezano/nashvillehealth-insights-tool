NASHVILLEHEALTH LOCAL PROTOTYPE - V6

1. Extract this ZIP to a normal folder.
2. Open Ollama and leave it running.
3. Double-click run_windows.bat.
4. The first launch creates a private Python environment and installs the packages.
5. Streamlit opens at http://localhost:8501.
6. Keep the command window open while testing. Press Ctrl+C there when finished.

TEST QUESTIONS
- Are Nashvillians living shorter lives than people in similar cities?
- How has life expectancy changed in Nashville since 2015?
- How did Nashville compare on adult smoking in 2023?
- How does Nashville compare with Austin and Denver on life expectancy?
- Why does housing affordability matter for health in Nashville?

Ask multiple follow-up questions, then download the PDF brief. Each answer should appear on its own page.

Later launches can use run_windows_quick.bat.
