# Testing Guide

## Landing page

Confirm that the Ask tab shows:

- NashvilleHealth logo
- Dark Nashville hero with Tennessee map and Davidson County highlight
- "Understanding Nashville's Health Through Data"
- The list of nine supported communities
- The question box without requiring a large initial scroll
- Three example questions

## Browse Topics

Confirm that:

- The topic-area dropdown is dark NashvilleHealth blue
- Four topic cards can appear across on a wide screen
- Cards are compact and readable
- Choosing Explore opens the Ask tab and runs a comparison question

## Test questions

```text
Are Nashvillians living shorter lives than people in similar cities?
```
Expected: Life Expectancy comparison.

```text
How has life expectancy changed in Nashville since 2015?
```
Expected: trend answer explaining that each multi-year point summarizes the full date range.

```text
How did Nashville compare on adult smoking in 2023?
```
Expected: 18.5%, peer average 13.8%, rank 9 of 9, and a correct worse-than interpretation.

```text
How does Nashville compare with Austin and Denver on life expectancy?
```
Expected: Nashville, Austin, and Denver only.

```text
Why does housing affordability matter for health in Nashville?
```
Expected: plain-language explanation, comparison, practical next steps, and local resources.

## Multi-question PDF

1. Ask at least two questions in the same conversation.
2. Download the PDF brief.
3. Confirm that each question appears on its own page.
4. Confirm that every page includes a main finding, key numbers, chart when available, context, next steps, and local resources.
5. Confirm that no text is clipped and no bottom source/disclaimer paragraph appears.
